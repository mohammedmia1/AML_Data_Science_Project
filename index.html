Precision and recall as review capacity increases

PrecisionRecall

Precision is the share of reviewed records with positive synthetic labels; recall is the share of all positive labels captured. As the queue grows, recall generally increases while precision tends to decrease. Both curves share a 0–100% scale.

How I approached the problem

01 · Prepare

Summarize synthetic transaction activity into one account-day record.

02 · Compare

Train logistic regression and XGBoost on earlier dates.

03 · Prioritize

Choose a review policy using validation data, before looking at test outcomes.

04 · Explain

Evaluate a later test period, examine SHAP explanations, and inspect drift.

Evidence & reproducibility

One model run is the source of truth for the notebook, review-capacity chart, and downloadable Tableau-ready export. The website reads saved test predictions; it does not train a model in the browser.

Frozen evaluation

1,534,403 training rows; 450,244 validation rows; 494,707 held-out test rows. Model selection and review policy were established before test evaluation.

Ranking performance

ROC-AUC 0.8523; PR-AUC 0.1214; 389 synthetic positives in a queue of 2,747 account-days (14.16% precision; 25.95% recall; 46.73× lift).

Reproduce the result

Fixed seed 42; nine frozen predictors; XGBoost 3.1.3; serialized model, exact environment versions, source-data checksum, and a runnable canonical pipeline.

Canonical run report · Canonical notebook

What this does not claim

This is a synthetic portfolio exercise, not a bank deployment. Model scores are not proven calibrated probabilities. Investigation costs are illustrative. A later monitoring population contains 922 positive labels among 923 records and cannot demonstrate ordinary production generalization.
