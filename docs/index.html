Why the sudden jump?

At rank 12, one positive-labeled account adds $52.76B across 169,756 historical USD transactions. That is 94.44% of all eligible positive-associated volume, so the amount rises early and then changes slowly.

Definitions, history window, and source checks
READ THIS BEFORE INTERPRETING DOLLARS

One ranking. Two different units of analysis.

The model ranks account-days. Transaction volume is calculated from the distinct accounts represented in that ranking, using their available history through the fixed review snapshot. It describes the scale of activity available for review.

What counts toward each amount?

Total volume includes every eligible source transaction linked to at least one selected account. Positive-associated volume includes those linked to at least one account with a selected positive account-day. A transaction enters each measure only once. A positive-associated account can have both legitimate and laundering-labeled transactions.

History, currencies, and double counting

History runs September 1–10, 2022, inclusive, as observed at the September 10 end-of-day snapshot. Only transactions with both payment and receiving currencies equal to US Dollar are included; the amount paid is used. Each original source row is treated as one transaction, including genuine repeated rows. A transfer involving two selected accounts is counted once. These histories must not be interpreted as information already known at an earlier alert date.

Denominators and customer identity

Total-volume coverage divides by all eligible unique USD activity linked to any account in the 494,707-account-day test population. Positive-volume coverage divides by eligible USD activity linked to any account with a positive account-day in that test population. Accounts are not assumed to be customers: no customer-to-account mapping is available. Review counts and F1 remain at account-day level.

Source and limitations

IBM synthetic HI-Small transactions and the canonical 389-positive ranking at the original 2,747 cutoff. Raw account-day labels were reconciled against all 494,707 ranking rows. All 494,707 cutoffs were checked for maximum F1; the interactive view displays exact results through 15,000. No model was retrained. Amounts are synthetic transaction turnover, not customer balances, recovered funds, or measured review effort. Positive labels are available for retrospective evaluation and would not be known when initially selecting a real queue.
